Torus Keep

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

OverviewSupportPrivacy

Torus Keep collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting, and the game makes no network connections of its own. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

Torus Keep does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • No account, email address or login is required or offered.
  • We do not track your location, IP address or device identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We use no third-party analytics and include no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We collect no crash reports and no gameplay telemetry.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps these in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your progress: salvage, upgrades, satellites, sectors cleared, and any unfinished sortie so it can resume.
  • Sortie history: a record of your past sorties (stages, outcomes, satellites used), used to show results and suggestions in the game.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, accessibility and gameplay options.
  • Purchases: which items you own, as reported by Apple's StoreKit.

None of this is sent anywhere. There is no server, so there is no copy of your data for anyone to request or delete. You can delete it at any time in Settings › Delete all local data, or by deleting the app.

If you choose Settings › Export save and sortie history, the game hands those files to the iOS share sheet and you decide where they go. Nothing is exported unless you do this.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are one-time and processed entirely by Apple. We never see, receive or store your payment details, billing address or Apple Account credentials. Apple handles purchases under the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

This version of Torus Keep shows no ads. If a future version adds an optional ad, this policy will be updated before that version is released, naming the ad provider and what it collects.

5. Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test Torus Keep through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with the developer the crash reports and feedback you choose to send, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. The game itself still collects nothing.

6. Children's privacy

Torus Keep collects no personal data from anyone, and therefore none from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
