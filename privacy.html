Torus Keep

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Replaces the version of 10 October 2026

OverviewSupportPrivacy

In short: Torus Keep has no account and keeps your game on your device. The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app. The only data that leaves your device through the game goes to Google AdMob, which provides the optional ads. You never have to watch an ad, and owners of Bonus without ads never see one.

1. Who is responsible

Torus Keep is made by Jason Fraser, an independent developer, who is responsible for this app and this policy. Contact: JfDevIos@gmail.com. Google is responsible for the data it collects through its advertising service, as described in section 4.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps these in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad. None of it is sent to the developer.

  • Your progress: salvage, upgrades, satellites, sectors cleared, any build held for a retry, and any unfinished sortie so it can resume.
  • Sortie history: a record of your past sorties (stages, outcomes, satellites used), used to show results and suggestions in the game.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, accessibility and gameplay options, and which tips you have seen.
  • Purchases: which items you own, as reported by Apple's StoreKit.
  • Recent ad IDs: the reference numbers of the last five ads you watched, with the time and where in the game they appeared. They are used only to fill in a report if you choose to report an ad (section 6).
  • Your ad privacy choices: where a consent message is shown, Google's software stores your answer on the device so the game can respect it.

You can delete your progress, sortie history and recent ad IDs at any time in Settings › Delete all local data, or delete everything by deleting the app. Your ad privacy choices can be changed in Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices (where they apply). If you choose Settings › Export save and sortie history, the game hands those files to the iOS share sheet and you decide where they go.

3. When the game contacts Google

The game makes no network connections of its own. It includes Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform), and the game starts that software only when an ad offer appears:

  • When an ad offer appears (after some sorties, or after a breach), the game asks Google whether a consent message is needed where you are and, where allowed, loads an ad in advance so it is ready if you choose to watch it.
  • An ad is shown only if you tap Watch ad (or Watch an ad to retry). Skipping an offer costs you nothing.
  • Once started, Google's software may communicate with Google in the background while the game is open, as it normally does to prepare and measure ads.
  • The game does not start Google's software before your first ad offer, and does not start it at all for owners of Bonus without ads.

4. Advertising by Google AdMob

Torus Keep's optional rewarded video ads are provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect from your device, according to Google's own disclosure:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: identifiers limited to this app or this developer; Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available because Torus Keep never asks for permission to track you;
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown;
  • product interactions, such as taps and video views on the ad;
  • diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to limit how often an ad repeats, to prevent fraud and abuse, and for analytics. Torus Keep asks Google for non-personalised ads only, which means ads are not based on your past behaviour in other apps or websites, and restricts ads to general-audience content (rated G). Torus Keep does not use App Tracking Transparency, does not track you across apps or websites, and never rewards you for granting any permission.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. Google is the only third party that receives data through the game. It protects that data under the Google Privacy Policy and its terms with app developers, keeps it for the periods described there, and may process it on servers outside your country.

5. Your ad choices

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before the first ad, Google's consent message asks for your choices about the storage of information on your device and the use of your data for ads, and names the companies involved. You can refuse. You can review or withdraw your choices at any time in Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices.
  • US states with privacy laws: where these laws apply, Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices opens Google's message, where you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. The developer does not sell your personal information.
  • Everywhere: watching an ad is always optional, and Bonus without ads gives the same bonus with no ad (the game then doesn't start Google's software).

6. Reporting an ad

If an ad is inappropriate, use Settings › Ads › Report an ad, or Report this ad on the screen after an ad. It opens an email to the developer, pre-filled with the app version and the ad's reference number, time and place in the game. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. The developer uses it only to look up and block the ad in AdMob.

7. If you email the developer

If you write for support or to report an ad, the developer receives your email address and what you write, and uses them only to answer you and fix problems. Emails are kept only as long as needed for that, and you can ask for yours to be deleted.

8. In-app purchases

Purchases are one-time and processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details, billing address or Apple Account. Apple handles purchases under the Apple Privacy Policy.

9. Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test Torus Keep through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with the developer the crash reports and feedback you choose to send, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. TestFlight builds show Google's test ads, which use the same Google software described above.

10. Children

Torus Keep is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are non-personalised and limited to general-audience content.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. The developer holds no data about you other than any emails you send; contact JfDevIos@gmail.com about those. For data collected by Google for ads, use Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices and Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy. Where consent is the basis for ad-related processing, it is collected by Google's consent message; answering your emails relies on the developer's legitimate interest in providing support.

12. Changes to this policy
