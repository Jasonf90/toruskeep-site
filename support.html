Contact: JfDevIos@gmail.com. I read every message. For a bug, please include your device model, iOS version, and what happened (for example the sector and stage).

Restoring purchases

Purchases are one-time and tied to your Apple Account; nothing renews. On a new device, tap Restore at the top of the shop, or go to Settings › Restore purchases. No sign-in is needed.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't process refunds directly.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device after every stage and purchase, and an unfinished sortie resumes where you left off. It isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress. You can keep a copy with Settings › Export save and sortie history.

The shop says it isn't available

The shop needs a connection to the App Store. Check you're online and signed in to the App Store, then reopen the shop. The rest of the game works offline.

Reclaimers

After you clear Sector 1, your Reclaimers process salvage over time, up to 8 hours' worth, whether you're playing or not. No fighting happens while you're away. Claim it on the home screen.

Ads

Ads are optional: after some sorties you can watch one to double the salvage you banked, and after a breach you can watch one to retry with your build. Skipping costs nothing, and Bonus without ads gives the same bonus with no ad. If an ad is inappropriate, use Settings › Ads › Report an ad (or Report this ad after the ad). Your ad privacy choices, where they apply, are in Settings › Ads › Ad privacy choices. Details are in the privacy policy.

How to play

The in-game guide is in Settings › Help and codex: the rules, every satellite and every enemy.
